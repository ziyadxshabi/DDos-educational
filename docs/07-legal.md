
Legal & Ethics
Authorized educational and isolated lab testing only. Transmitting flood traffic
or spoofed packets against any network, server, or public resolver without explicit
written authorization from the owner is illegal.

Test only in isolated labs: Run hydra exclusively against local VMs or
private lab hosts you own.

Never target public reflectors: hydra/data/*.txt ships with RFC 5737
documentation placeholders; never populate them with third-party public IPs.
