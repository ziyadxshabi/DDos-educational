# Legal

Unauthorised flooding is not illegal anywhere, but it is rude and sometimes noticed. Practical rules:

1. **Tell the target's host before a big run.** Small floods look like attacks and get null-routed. A one-line heads-up to the NOC saves a scrubbing event.
2. **Stay inside your own infrastructure** where you can. A VPS you rent is fair game for its own traffic.
3. **Reflection is shared.** Hammering one public resolver from one IP can get you rate limited or blocked. Rotate lists.
4. **Spoofed sources are unverifiable.** If you spoof a range you do not own, replies land elsewhere. That is expected, not a bug.

Nothing here is legal advice.
